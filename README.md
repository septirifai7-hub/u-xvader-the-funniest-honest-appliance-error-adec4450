## The entry

> Error E04: drum will not spin. One sock is missing. I have searched the entire load twice and I am no longer confident the sock was ever real.

A washing machine, speaking in its own flat voice, reports a drum that will not spin and a sock that has vanished. The laugh comes from the machine's honesty: it did look, twice, and the sock is simply gone.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the error line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), as a single line with no trailing newline.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```
